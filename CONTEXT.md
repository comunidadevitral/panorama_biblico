# Panorama Bíblico Context

Glossary of domain-specific terms for the Panorama Bíblico website, which organizes and presents BibleProject videos for studying the Bible by book and literary genre.

## Language

**Testamento**:
A major division of the Bible: either Antigo Testamento (AT) or Novo Testamento (NT).
_Avoid_: Testament (when English is preferred), Treaty, Covenant

**Livro**:
An individual book of the Bible, such as Gênesis, Isaías, Mateus, or Romanos.
_Avoid_: Book (when English is preferred), Writing, Scroll

**Categoria**:
A grouping of Livros that share common characteristics (e.g., Pentateuco, Históricos, Poéticos e Sabedoria, Profetas for AT; Evangelhos, Histórico, Epístolas Paulinas, Epístolas Pastorais, Epístolas Gerais, Revelação for NT; Introdução, Narrativa Bíblica, Poesia Bíblica, Literatura Sapiencial, Profecia e Apocalíptico, Cartas e Evangelhos for "Como Ler a Bíblia").
_Avoid_: Category (when English is preferred), Group, Classification

**Vídeo**:
A BibleProject video that explains a Livro do Testamento or a concept from the "Como Ler a Bíblia" series.
_Avoid_: Video (when English is preferred), Film, Clip

**Visão Geral**:
A special Vídeo that provides an overview of either the Antigo Testamento or Novo Testamento as a whole, rather than focusing on a single Livro. Marked by the overview: true property in the video data.
_Avoid_: Overview (when English is preferred), Summary, Introduction

**Como Ler a Bíblia**:
A special series of Vídeos that teaches how to read and interpret different literary genres found in the Bible.
_Avoid_: How to Read the Bible (when English is preferred), Biblical Interpretation Guide, Hermeneutics Series

**Gênero Literário**:
A category of writing style within the Bible, such as Narrativa Bíblica, Poesia Bíblica, Literatura Sapiencial, Profecia e Apocalíptico, or Cartas e Evangelhos, used to organize the "Como Ler a Bíblia" series.
_Avoid_: Literary Genre (when English is preferred), Writing Style, Text Type

**Número**:
The sequential identifier assigned to a Livro within the Christian Bible canon (1-66), used for ordering and reference. In the video data, this corresponds to the 'number' field, with 0 reserved for overview videos.
_Avoid_: Number (when English is preferred), Ordinal, Canonical Number

**Slug**:
A short, URL-friendly identifier used internally for routing and categorization (e.g., 'genesis', 'salmos', 'pentateuco').
_Avoid_: Slug (when English is preferred), Identifier, Key

**Categoria do Vídeo**:
The specific Categoria to which a Vídeo belongs, displayed on video cards for user navigation.
_Avoid_: Video Category (when English is preferred), Group, Type

**Testamento da Categoria**:
Indicates whether a Categoria belongs to AT (Antigo Testamento), NT (Novo Testamento), or 'como-ler' (Como Ler a Bíblia series), used for organizing content sections.
_Avoid_: Category Testament (when English is preferred), Section, Division
